## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — Rim War** is a powerful tool built to help you experience large-scale faction warfare and advanced diplomacy systems in your RimWorld colony. Watch factions battle for territory, form alliances, and wage war across the planet — all without your colony's direct involvement.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — Rim War** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their RimWorld modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Faction Wars | Factions attack each other's settlements and compete for territory |
| Alliance System | Factions form alliances and declare war based on diplomatic relations |
| Settlement Battles | Witness sieges and battles between AI factions on the world map |
| Diplomacy Events | New events, treaties, and diplomatic actions for faction interactions |
